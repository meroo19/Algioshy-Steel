About Us

Excellence in Steel Manufacturing

Algioshy Steel is a leading steel manufacturing company based in Egypt, committed to delivering high-quality steel products and exceptional service to our clients. With state-of-the-art facilities and a dedicated team, we have established ourselves as a trusted name in the industry.

Under the leadership of ENG. ISLAM ALGIOSHY, our CEO, we continue to innovate and expand our capabilities to meet the evolving needs of the construction and manufacturing sectors.

Our commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction has made us a preferred partner for major construction projects across Egypt and beyond.
